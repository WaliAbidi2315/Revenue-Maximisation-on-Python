Business diagnosis

Three findings shape the strategy

Retention risk

Customer attrition is material

6,620 of 16,825 FY2023 customers had not placed an order by FY2025. The report associates $431.4M in historical revenue with these churned accounts.

Concentration

A small base drives most revenue

2,642 customers, or 8.6% of the base, account for 80% of total revenue, making key-account protection a priority.

Cross-sell

Product expansion opportunity

15,862 customers purchased Consumables only. The report estimates $136.9M annual upside at a 5% conversion assumption.

Analytical workflow

From raw transactions to strategic decisions

01 · Prepare

Data quality and feature engineering

Standardise field names and types, correct fiscal-year assignments, aggregate duplicate order/SKU lines, handle invalid records and derive unit price and revenue bands.

02 · Explore

Revenue, seasonality and geography

Analyse fiscal-year trends, monthly peaks, regional order/revenue patterns, product class mix and log-log OLS relationships.

03 · Understand customers

Churn, cohorts and RFM

Use customer Pareto analysis, churn movement, cohort retention and K-means RFM segmentation to identify valuable, at-risk and lost accounts.

04 · Assess portfolio

Products, pricing and cross-sell

Examine BCG division categories, SKU concentration, price–quantity patterns and the Consumables-to-Instruments cross-sell gap.

05 · Forecast

Business-as-usual baseline

Fit a Facebook Prophet model to monthly revenue and use its forecast as the baseline against which proposed strategies are assessed.

06 · Recommend

Quantify seven growth levers

Translate the analysis into account protection, churn recovery, cross-sell, portfolio allocation, seasonal readiness, SKU rationalisation and regional improvement.
